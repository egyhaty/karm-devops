Made for learning, not memorising

You do not need to know Kubernetes before you begin. If a lesson feels unfamiliar, use its “Start here” box and follow the small steps. The Arabic recap is there to make the main point quick to review; the full lesson and interview practice are in English.

This is the first path in a growing project library. New projects can have their own lessons, examples, and practice without mixing their details into this Kubernetes path.
